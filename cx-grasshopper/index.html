Grasshopper
An Interactive Introduction to Gradient Descent

You are a grasshopper in a valley you cannot see.

Somewhere along this landscape there is a lowest point.
Reaching it is the whole of the job.
The trouble is your eyesight.

You can see the ground for a few centimetres around your feet and nothing further.
From that patch you choose two things: which way to jump, and how far.
Then you land somewhere new, look down again, and choose again.
Get as low as the landscape allows and plant the flag.
